## Quy Tac

- System font, khong scale font theo viewport.
- Radius compact 8-12 px; avatar co the tron.
- Khong dung mau lam dau hieu duy nhat; kem icon/label.
- Motion chi phuc vu keyboard, navigation hoac feedback thao tac.
- Safe-area lay tu `react-native-safe-area-context`, khong dung gia tri hinh hoc co dinh.
